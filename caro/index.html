      Cờ Caro & Gomoku

      How to play

      Goal

      Get five in a row — horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

      Rules

      • Gomoku (freestyle): five or more in a row wins. With “Exact five only”, six or more does not count.
      • Caro (Blocked-End Rule): a five only wins if not both ends are blocked by the opponent. Optionally the board edge also counts as a block.

      Touch

      • Tap to place (with tap-to-confirm: tap again or press ✓).
      • Drag to pan, pinch to zoom.
      • Double-tap an occupied spot or outside the board to zoom in.

      Mouse & keyboard

      • Click to place, drag to pan, wheel to zoom.
      • ←↑→↓ move cursor, Enter/Space place, +/- zoom, F fit, U undo, Esc close dialog.

      Sound

      Audio starts after your first tap. On iPhone, the hardware silent switch may mute web audio.

      Install

      On iPhone/iPad: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android/desktop Chrome use “Install app” in the menu.
